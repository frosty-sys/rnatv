RNA. TV is a fast, open-source launcher and app runtime designed for Android TV and Google TV. It brings your apps, web browsing, AI tools, and phone remote together on your television, with D-Pad-first navigation and local profiles.

FEATURES

• TV launcher: Customize one-row or two-row app docks, browse installed apps, switch between recent apps, and use multiple offline profiles.
• Remote remapping: Assign single, double, triple, and long button presses to supported apps and actions.
• TV browser: Browse with a D-Pad-friendly on-screen keyboard, full-screen mode, bookmarks, and website shortcuts. Browser behavior and content depend on the sites you visit.
• AI assistant: Access AI chat and supported assistant actions, with free keyless provider options and optional user-provided keys. AI availability depends on the selected provider and network connection.
• Phone controller: Pair a phone with the TV over the same local Wi-Fi network for remote, D-Pad, text-entry, and gamepad-style controls.
• Wallpapers: Choose from 43+ bundled 4K wallpapers, your own photos, and supported live wallpapers.
• TV keyboard: Use the built-in D-Pad keyboard, including its system keyboard service where supported.
• Control Panel: Configure launcher behavior, performance mode, updates, sleep timers, blue-light warmth, and other supported settings.

PRIVACY AND PERMISSIONS

Profiles, preferences, and supported app data are stored locally on the device. Some features require permissions such as Accessibility, Display over other apps, Usage Access, media access, or permission to install updates. Network access is used for browsing, downloads, updates, phone-controller communication, and online AI providers. Use of third-party websites and AI services is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

REQUIREMENTS

Android 10 (API 29) or later is the project's documented minimum. Some features and settings vary by device, Android version, and TV manufacturer. A D-Pad remote is recommended. An internet connection is needed for online features; local launcher features can be used offline.
